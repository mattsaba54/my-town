Flint, Michigan

Flint is a city located in Genesee County, Michigan, and it is also the county seat.

Flint played an important role in the history of the automobile industry. General Motors was founded in Flint in 1908, which helped the city become a major center for automobile manufacturing.

The city is also home to the University of Michigan-Flint, which was established in 1956 and is part of the University of Michigan system.

Flint has several cultural and educational attractions, including the Flint Institute of Arts and the Sloan Museum of Discovery.

The Flint River runs through the city and has been an important part of the area's history and development.
