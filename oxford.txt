Oxford, Michigan

Oxford is a small village located in Oakland County, Michigan.

The Oxford area was first settled in the early 1830s, and the Village of Oxford was officially incorporated in 1876.

Oxford has a historic downtown area with local restaurants, shops, and businesses that give the community a small-town feel.

The area also has many lakes and outdoor spaces. Stoney Lake was one of the larger natural lakes mentioned in Oxford's early history.

Oxford also has an interesting industrial history. Gravel mining became an important industry in the area during the early 1900s, and by 1924 several of Michigan's largest gravel pits were located near Oxford.
